# Frequently Asked Questions

### Do your website plans include domain registration and hosting?
No. You purchase the domain and hosting accounts directly to maintain full legal ownership. We assist with configuration and DNS routing to production platforms (such as Vercel, Netlify, or standard VPS setups) at no extra charge.

### What are the payment terms for website projects?
We operate on a milestone structure: a 50% upfront deposit to initiate development sprints, and the remaining 50% upon final client sign-off prior to production deployment and source code transfer.

### What if I need changes after the website goes live?
All website plans include a post-launch support window (ranging from 7 to 30 days depending on the plan tier) to resolve any bugs or formatting discrepancies. Minor text or image updates are included; major feature expansions or structural additions are billed at an agreed-upon hourly or project rate.

### How do revisions work during the project?
Each plan includes dedicated revision rounds (for example, up to 3 structured rounds in the Professional plan). Feedback is collected via structured comments to iterate cleanly without delaying agreed launch dates.

### Can Meridian Dynamics manage my paid advertising?
Our standard packages focus strictly on organic content growth and web development. Paid ad campaign setup and management can be arranged as an add-on or custom retainer, with all ad spend billed directly to your ad accounts.
