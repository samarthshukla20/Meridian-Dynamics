# Meridian Dynamics — Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you sell fixed website plans or packages?

No. Meridian Dynamics does not sell predefined website plans or fixed packages. Each offer is designed around the client's goals, requirements, business context, and budget. We first understand what the client needs, then propose a suitable scope and custom quote.

### How much does a website cost?

There is no single fixed price because projects are scoped individually. The quote depends on the type of website, design complexity, functionality, integrations, content requirements, and any additional services the client needs. Invite the visitor to share their requirements so the team can prepare a tailored offer.

### What services does Meridian Dynamics provide?

Meridian Dynamics works on websites, digital products, web engineering, and growth infrastructure. Depending on the client's goals, a project may include responsive web design and development, local search visibility, Google Business Profile support, conversion tracking, or social content. Exact deliverables are agreed individually.

### Can Meridian help with Google Business Profile and local SEO?

Yes, these are capability areas referenced on the website. The appropriate work depends on the business type, service area, current online presence, and goals. Ask a few relevant questions before suggesting a custom scope.

### How long will my project take?

Timelines depend on requirements, content readiness, complexity, and review cycles. The team confirms a realistic timeline after understanding the scope.

### Do you provide domain registration and hosting?

Domain and hosting arrangements are discussed as part of the project scope. Confirm the deployment setup, third-party subscriptions, ownership, and billing in the proposal rather than assuming they are included.

### Can you manage paid advertising?

Paid advertising requirements should be discussed with the team and confirmed in the custom proposal. Do not assume ad spend or campaign management is included in another service.

### Do you offer social media services?

Discuss the client's goals, platforms, content needs, and preferred cadence with the team. Meridian Dynamics creates a custom offer based on the agreed scope rather than selling a fixed public plan.

Use the contact form at https://meridiandynamics.online/ or email meridiandynamics.online@gmail.com. You can also call +91 87993 12003. Share a short description of the business, the outcome you're looking for, and any requirements or constraints; the team can then discuss a tailored offer.

### Can you take on custom requirements?

The team can review custom requirements and confirm feasibility, scope, timeline, and pricing before making a commitment.
