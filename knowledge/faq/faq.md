# Meridian Dynamics — Frequently Asked Questions

### What does Meridian Dynamics do?

Meridian Dynamics works on websites, digital products, web engineering, and digital growth. Depending on a business's needs, relevant work may include responsive web design and development, local search visibility, Google Business Profile support, conversion tracking, analytics, or social content.

### I need a website. Where should I start?

Start by sharing what your business does, who the website needs to reach, and what you want visitors to do—for example, enquire, book, browse services, or learn about your business. If you already have a website, share what you would like to improve.

### How can you help my business attract more customers?

The right approach depends on how customers currently discover and evaluate your business. Relevant areas may include improving the website journey, strengthening local search visibility, improving your Google Business Profile, or creating more purposeful social content. Tell us what kind of business you run and what you want to improve so we can discuss useful next steps.

### Can Meridian help with Google Business Profile and local SEO?

Yes. These are capability areas referenced by Meridian Dynamics. Potential work depends on the business type, service area, current online presence, and goals. Share your business category and location to help identify relevant opportunities. Do not promise a specific ranking or result.

### Do you provide social media services?

Social content and related work can be discussed based on your goals, platforms, content needs, and preferred publishing cadence. Share which platforms you use and what you want to achieve.

### How much does a website or digital project cost?

Project pricing depends on the requirements, such as the type of website, design complexity, functionality, integrations, content needs, and any related services. Meridian Dynamics prepares offers around the project rather than selling predefined packages. To discuss an estimate, share what you need and your approximate budget if you have one.

### Do you sell fixed plans or packages?

No. Meridian Dynamics does not sell predefined website plans or fixed service packages. The proposed scope is based on the client's requirements and priorities.

### How long will my project take?

Timelines depend on requirements, content readiness, complexity, and review cycles. The team confirms a realistic timeline after understanding the project scope.

### Do you provide domain registration and hosting?

Domain and hosting arrangements are discussed during project scoping. Confirm the deployment setup, ownership, subscriptions, and billing with the team rather than assuming these are included.

### Can you manage paid advertising?

Paid advertising requirements should be discussed with the team and confirmed in the project proposal. Do not assume ad spend or campaign management is included in another service.

### Can you handle custom technical requirements?

The team can review the requirements and confirm feasibility, scope, timeline, and pricing before making a commitment.
