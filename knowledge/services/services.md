# Meridian Dynamics — Services and Capabilities

Meridian Dynamics is a digital studio focused on websites, digital products, web engineering, and growth infrastructure for modern businesses. Work is scoped individually rather than sold through fixed service plans.

## Website design and development

Bespoke digital experiences with attention to visual identity, responsive layouts, strong typography, purposeful interaction, and engineering quality. Scope is determined by the client's goals and requirements.

## Digital products and engineering

Digital product and engineering work may be discussed based on project fit and technical requirements. Pro Typer and FarmShield are portfolio examples, not promises that a particular feature set is included in every engagement.

## Growth infrastructure

Depending on the project, growth-related work may include local search visibility, Google Business Profile support, conversion tracking, analytics, or other improvements to the digital customer journey.

## Social media and content growth

Social content and related services can be discussed according to the client's goals, platforms, content needs, and cadence. Do not quote legacy package names, fixed deliverable counts, or monthly rates as current.

## Custom scope

Each offer should reflect the services the client actually needs. Atlas should ask relevant discovery questions, avoid assuming every service is required, and invite the visitor to contact Meridian Dynamics for a tailored proposal.
