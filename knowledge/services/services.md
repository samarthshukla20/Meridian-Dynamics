# Meridian Dynamics — Services and Capabilities

Meridian Dynamics is a digital studio focused on websites, digital products, web engineering, and growth infrastructure for modern businesses. The most relevant approach depends on the business's goals and current needs.

## Website design and development

We create digital experiences with attention to visual identity, responsive layouts, strong typography, purposeful interaction, and engineering quality.

This may be relevant when a business needs a new website, wants to improve an existing one, or wants to make it easier for visitors to understand its services and take the next step.

## Digital products and web engineering

Digital product and engineering work can be discussed based on project fit and technical requirements. Pro Typer and FarmShield are portfolio examples; they should not be treated as promises that a particular feature set is included in every engagement.

## Local search and Google Business Profile

Support in these areas may help a business improve how it appears to potential local customers. Relevant work depends on the business category, service area, current online presence, and goals. Do not promise a specific ranking or outcome.

## Analytics and conversion tracking

Where included in the agreed scope, analytics and conversion tracking can help a business understand how visitors interact with its digital presence and which actions are being completed. Do not claim tracking is already configured for a particular client unless verified.

## Social media and content

Social content and related work can be discussed according to the business's goals, platforms, content needs, and publishing cadence. Do not quote legacy package names, fixed deliverable counts, or monthly rates as current.

## Helping a client choose a starting point

Visitors do not need to know which service they need before contacting Meridian Dynamics. Ask what kind of business they run and what they want to improve—for example, their website, local visibility, or social media presence. Then explain the most relevant capabilities supported by the available information.

Do not list every service in every answer. Select the capabilities that best match the user's question and explain them in plain language.
