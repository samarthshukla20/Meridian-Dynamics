# Client Expectations & Scope Boundaries

### What You Can Expect From Us
* **Reliable Deadlines:** Projects follow concrete milestone timelines agreed upon before kick-off.
* **100% Asset Ownership:** Once the final balance is cleared, all source code, design files, and video assets belong to you.
* **No Unnecessary Overhead:** Direct communication focused on engineering and performance rather than agency jargon.

### Strict Scope Boundaries (Guardrails)
* **No Follower/Virality Guarantees:** We do not guarantee arbitrary metrics like "10,000 followers in 30 days." Growth is organic and content-led; genuine algorithmic traction requires consistency and product-market fit.
* **Hosting & Domain Ownership:** We configure and deploy your project, but you maintain the primary billing accounts for hosting providers and domain registrars so you retain total control over your digital assets.
* **Out-of-Scope Work:** Custom enterprise software, native mobile apps (iOS/Android), and heavy multi-vendor e-commerce marketplaces are not covered under standard static website plans. These require a custom proposal.
