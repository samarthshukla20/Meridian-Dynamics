# Social Growth Packages

### 1. Foundation Plan
* **Price:** ₹9,999 / month
* **Platforms Managed:** 1 Platform (Instagram or LinkedIn)
* **Target Audience:** Businesses that need an active, trustworthy profile without committing to video production.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * 12 custom-designed graphic posts per month (carousels, brand infographics, announcement cards).
  * Profile optimization (bio, call-to-action link structuring, story highlight covers).
  * Niche keyword and hashtag cluster strategy.
  * End-to-end caption copywriting.

### 2. Momentum Plan
* **Price:** ₹18,999 / month
* **Platforms Managed:** 2 Platforms (e.g., Instagram + LinkedIn or Instagram + Facebook)
* **Target Audience:** Fast-growing businesses seeking active reach and community engagement through short-form video.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * 16 custom branded graphic posts per month.
  * 8 short-form vertical video assets (Reels / Shorts) with dynamic subtitles and audio synchronization.
  * Monthly content calendar delivered 5 days prior to month-start for review and sign-off.
  * Active community engagement during the first 60 minutes after every post to boost algorithmic reach.
  * Bi-weekly performance review tracking reach, saves, profile visits, and follower growth trends.

### 3. Dominance Plan
* **Price:** ₹32,999 / month
* **Platforms Managed:** Multi-platform omni-channel (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts, X)
* **Target Audience:** Established brands and founders seeking industry authority and aggressive inbound audience acquisition.
* **Key Deliverables:**
  * 24 high-retention graphic posts per month.
  * 16 short-form vertical videos (scripting hooks, editing, captions, and visual retention hooks).
  * Full inbound direct message (DM) and comment triage (routing qualified business leads directly to the founder/sales team).
  * Weekly strategy check-in and content pivot reviews.
  * Dedicated monthly creative sprint.

### Critical Service Boundary
* Ad spend budgets are paid directly by the client to advertising platforms (Meta, Google, LinkedIn).
* Meridian Dynamics executes strictly organic, content-led growth. We do not use engagement pods or automated follower bots.
