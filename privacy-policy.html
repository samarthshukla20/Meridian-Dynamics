01 / OVERVIEW

Privacy should be understood.

Meridian Dynamics (“we”, “our” or “us”) aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared and retained when you visit meridiandynamics.online, submit an inquiry, or use our AI-powered chatbot, Meridian Atlas.

Meridian Dynamics currently operates as an individual-led team and is not a registered company. For privacy questions or requests, contact meridiandynamics.online@gmail.com.

Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, simply using the website does not replace that consent.

02 / INFORMATION

What information we handle.

Information you provide

When you submit our contact form, the form may collect your name, email address, message, and the optional business or industry information you choose to provide.

Information handled by the chatbot

When you use Meridian Atlas, the text you submit is processed by our backend and sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. Relevant information from our business knowledge base may be included to provide context.

Analytics information

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with the website. Depending on the configuration, analytics may process pages viewed, interactions, referring pages, browser and device details, online identifiers, and approximate location inferred from technical information.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment-card details, sensitive personal information, or confidential information through the contact form or chatbot unless it is genuinely necessary and you have an appropriate basis to share it.

03 / PURPOSE

How information is used.

  • Responding to inquiries and communicating with prospective or existing clients.
  • Understanding project requirements and discussing possible services.
  • Operating, maintaining, and troubleshooting the website and chatbot.
  • Generating AI chatbot responses and providing information about our services.
  • Measuring website usage and improving content and usability.
  • Protecting the website and services against misuse, spam, and technical issues.
  • Addressing legitimate business needs and applicable legal obligations.
04 / CONTACT FORMS

Your inquiry, handled carefully.

Our contact form uses Web3Forms to process form submissions and deliver them to our business email. Members of the Meridian Dynamics team can access these inquiries to respond and manage related business communications.

Web3Forms and any infrastructure or email-delivery providers it uses may process submission data and associated technical information under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not control the independent practices of those providers. Read the Web3Forms Privacy Policy for more details.

You may request deletion of a contact-form submission by emailing meridiandynamics.online@gmail.com. We will review the request and delete information under our control where appropriate, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention needs.

05 / ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

How Meridian Atlas uses AI.

Meridian Atlas is an AI-powered chatbot that answers questions about Meridian Dynamics and its services. Messages submitted to the chatbot are sent through our backend to Google's Gemini API. Relevant business knowledge-base content may be sent alongside a request to provide context.

We do not intentionally save chatbot conversations in a persistent conversation-history database. This does not mean that technical logs, temporary records, or provider-side records cannot exist. Actual handling and retention depend partly on the applicable provider terms and configuration.

AI responses may be incomplete or inaccurate. Verify important details directly with Meridian Dynamics before relying on them for a business decision.

06 / ANALYTICS & COOKIES

Understanding how the site is used.

We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic and interactions. Depending on its implementation and configuration, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies.

Our website currently does not display a cookie-consent banner or provide dedicated accept/reject controls. We have not yet verified every cookie or browser-storage mechanism that may be used by analytics tags or service providers, nor have we confirmed the data-retention setting in our Google Analytics account.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect website functionality or analytics measurements, and browser controls may not prevent every form of processing.

Our next review: We will review our analytics and consent configuration and implement notices or controls required by applicable law. A privacy policy alone does not replace a legally required consent mechanism.

Learn more about Google Analytics' privacy and safeguarding practices.

07 / SERVICE PROVIDERS

When information is shared.

We do not intend to sell visitors' personal information. Information may be transmitted to or processed by the following services where needed to provide website functionality:

  • Web3Forms — contact-form processing and delivery.
  • Vercel — hosting and operation of our backend.
  • Google Gemini — processing chatbot prompts and generating answers.
  • Google Analytics — measuring website usage.

We may also disclose information where required by law, in response to valid legal requests, to protect rights or safety, or to address security incidents and misuse. Service providers may use relevant infrastructure providers under their own terms.

Review the Vercel Privacy Policy for information about Vercel's practices. Provider locations, precise logs, and retention arrangements have not all been independently verified by us.

08 / RETENTION

Keeping information only as needed.

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate business needs.

  • Contact-form submissions: kept while needed to respond to an inquiry, manage a business relationship, or address related matters; deletion can be requested by email.
  • Chatbot conversations: not intentionally stored in a persistent conversation-history database. Provider or technical logs may still exist, and their retention periods have not been verified.
  • Google Analytics: the retention setting in our Analytics account has not yet been confirmed.

Deleting information from systems under our control may not delete records held independently by a third-party provider. A separate request to that provider may be required.

09 / SECURITY

Reasonable care, not false promises.

We take reasonable steps intended to protect information handled through our website and business processes against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No website, transmission method, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

If you believe that information submitted through our website has been exposed or misused, contact meridiandynamics.online@gmail.com.

10 / INTERNATIONAL VISITORS

Information across borders.

Our website serves visitors in India and other countries. Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where a visitor resides. We have not independently verified every processing location or international-transfer arrangement used by our providers.

Such processing is subject to the providers' applicable terms and any legal requirements that apply to the processing.

11 / YOUR CHOICES

Your information, your voice.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or raise an objection or complaint regarding particular processing activities.

To make a request, email meridiandynamics.online@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject and describe what you need. We may need to verify your identity or ask for additional information before responding. We will review and respond in accordance with applicable law.

Some information may need to be retained where a legal obligation or valid exception applies. If your request concerns records held independently by Google, Web3Forms, Vercel, or another provider, we may direct you to that provider's privacy process where appropriate.

12 / CHILDREN

A general audience, not a children's service.

Our website is intended for a general audience, particularly individuals and businesses interested in digital services. It is not specifically designed or directed at children, and we do not intentionally seek personal information from children through the website.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the circumstances and take appropriate steps.

13 / EXTERNAL WEBSITES

Beyond our website.

Our website may link to social-media platforms, portfolios, and other external services. Those websites operate under their own terms and privacy practices. We encourage you to review their policies before submitting information. A link does not mean that Meridian Dynamics controls the linked service's data practices.

14 / UPDATES

This policy may evolve.

We may update this policy as our website, services, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will appear on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage visitors to review it periodically.
